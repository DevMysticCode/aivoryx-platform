# Aivoryx Business Operating Platform
## Initial Implementation Scope

### Purpose

We will develop a unified business platform that brings the company's key sales, field, HR and operational processes into one connected system.

The platform will be designed around the company's actual workflow rather than as a collection of disconnected software tools.

### Initial priorities

The first implementation will focus on two parallel business areas:

1. CRM and Sales Operations
2. HR Operations

CRM will be the primary operational stream because it connects directly to lead generation, telecalling, field sales, quotation and booking.

HR will be developed in parallel so that the internal employee-management workflow can begin moving into the new platform early.

---

# 1. CRM and Lead Management

The system will consolidate leads arriving from the company's existing sources, including:

- Tata
- IndiaMART
- Justdial
- Meta advertising
- Google advertising
- Company website

The system will provide a central lead record with source information, customer details, assignment, activities, follow-ups and status.

### Lead workflow

Lead source
→ Lead received
→ Validation and duplicate check
→ Telecaller assignment
→ Call / follow-up
→ Qualification
→ Field-agent assignment
→ Site visit
→ Survey
→ Design / BOQ
→ Quotation
→ Approval
→ Booking

The platform will also support appropriate outcomes such as follow-up, nurture, lost/disqualified and manual intervention.

---

# 2. Telecalling

The system will integrate with the company's telephony environment so that calls can remain connected to the relevant lead/customer record.

The CRM will maintain relevant call information such as:

- call activity
- agent
- date/time
- duration where available
- disposition
- notes
- follow-up
- recording reference where supported

This will allow management to evaluate telecalling activity and conversion performance.

---

# 3. Field Sales

Qualified leads will move into the field-sales workflow.

Field users will be able to access assigned work through a mobile-friendly installable web application.

The field workflow will include:

- assigned visits
- visit scheduling
- GPS
- check-in/check-out
- travel distance/KM
- site survey
- notes
- photos and supporting documents
- customer inputs
- visit outcome
- new leads generated by field staff

The initial approach will use a Progressive Web App so the field team can use the system in an app-like mobile experience without requiring a separate native mobile application at this stage.

---

# 4. Survey, Design and Quotation

The site survey will provide structured information needed for the next sales step.

The workflow will connect:

Survey
→ Design / DYO
→ BOQ
→ Quotation
→ Approval
→ Customer communication
→ Booking

This will reduce repeated data entry and manual handoffs between teams.

---

# 5. HR

HR will be developed in parallel.

The initial HR scope includes:

- employee master
- employee ID
- department
- designation
- branch
- joining date
- reporting manager
- employment status
- employee documents
- attendance
- field/GPS attendance where applicable
- leave application
- manager approval
- leave balance
- holiday calendar
- employee expenses
- approval workflow

The broader HR architecture will support later payroll, incentives, commissions, advances, reimbursements, performance and appraisal capabilities.

---

# 6. Unified Business Data

The system will use an integrated business data architecture so that relevant records remain connected.

For example:

Customer
→ Lead
→ Survey
→ Quotation
→ Booking
→ Project
→ Invoice / Payment
→ Service

Employee-related operational information will also remain connected to HR, attendance, field activity and performance data where applicable.

Management will be able to see information across departments without relying on separate spreadsheets and disconnected systems.

---

# 7. Automation

The platform will be designed around configurable workflows.

Examples include:

New lead
→ assign appropriate telecaller
→ create follow-up task
→ start response SLA
→ send communication where configured

Qualified lead
→ assign field agent
→ create visit

Survey completed
→ move to quotation workflow

Quotation approved
→ send quotation / notify responsible users

Employee created
→ create HR onboarding tasks
→ configure attendance access
→ assign reporting manager

Automation will be introduced progressively and will be traceable when an action fails.

---

# 8. Notifications and Communication

The platform will be designed to connect relevant communication to leads, customers, projects and employees.

Planned communication channels include, where applicable:

- WhatsApp
- SMS
- email
- push notifications
- cloud telephony

The exact external services will remain integrated through controlled adapters so that the core application is not tightly coupled to one provider.

---

# 9. Future Operational Modules

The architecture will allow the platform to expand into:

- project management
- procurement
- inventory
- logistics
- installation
- QC
- documentation
- net metering
- commissioning
- handover
- finance and accounts
- customer service
- warranty
- AMC
- partner/referral management

These areas will be introduced progressively after the initial CRM and HR workflows are established.

---

# 10. User Experience

The application will be designed as a modern, fast business application with:

- responsive desktop and mobile interfaces
- app-like mobile experience for field users
- simple navigation
- clear status and workflow information
- fast search and data access
- meaningful loading and empty states
- clear error messages
- role-based access
- secure access to company data

The goal is to minimize unnecessary clicks and manual coordination.

---

# 11. Support and Reliability

Important operations will have traceable system references.

If an operation fails, the user will receive a clear explanation where possible and a reference number that can be shared with the support/development team.

This will allow technical teams to trace failures through the application, integrations and background processes.

---

# 12. Migration and Transition

The new platform will be introduced progressively alongside the company's existing systems.

Existing data and integrations will be assessed before migration.

The transition approach will prioritize:
- active business data
- verified mappings
- test imports
- reconciliation
- user acceptance
- controlled production migration

The objective is to avoid disrupting ongoing business operations.

---

# 13. Product Direction

The platform will be developed specifically around the company's current business requirements while using reusable technology foundations.

The underlying architecture will remain suitable for future expansion of the company's operations and additional business capabilities.
